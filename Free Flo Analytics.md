## Inventory Optimization & Customer Analysis for Free Flo Box 

This project focused on helping a nonprofit organization improve inventory management and customer engagement across two locations. My role combined data analysis, visualization, and stakeholder collaboration to deliver actionable insights that aligned with the organization's mission.

### Objective
The nonprofit wanted to better understand inventory usage patterns and customer behavior to optimize stock levels and streamline distribution processes.

### My Approach

- **Stakeholder Collaboration:**  
  I worked closely with nonprofit stakeholders to gather domain-specific context and define key performance indicators. This helped ensure that the analysis aligned with both operational realities and strategic goals.

- **Data Cleaning & Preparation:**  
  I received large, messy datasets from two sites—each with different formatting. I used advanced Excel functions and VBA scripting to clean, normalize, and merge the data. This included handling missing values, standardizing product categories, and resolving inconsistencies.

- **Statistical & Quantitative Analysis:**  
  I applied statistical methods to uncover trends in customer demand and inventory movement. By segmenting customers and analyzing purchase frequencies, I was able to identify high- and low-demand products, seasonal fluctuations, and inefficiencies in stock turnover.

- **Data Visualization:**  
  To make the insights accessible, I built interactive Tableau dashboards. These visualizations highlighted inventory trends, demand spikes, and customer engagement metrics—allowing stakeholders to explore the data in real time and support informed decision-making.

- **Impact & Results:**  
  My analysis led to data-driven recommendations that helped the organization optimize stock levels, reduce waste, and better match inventory to community needs. The final deliverable included a structured analytical report and live dashboards, both aligned with the nonprofit’s operational and strategic objectives.
